What is saved?

The workbook is read in this tab. Selected rows are sent to the server to prepare and send emails; the app does not store the workbook or employee schedule rows. Refreshing or clearing the report removes the browser’s working copy.

The server stores an encrypted mailbox connection, department settings, staff permissions, temporary sign-in records, and minimal send receipts for duplicate protection. Receipts contain the sending staff member’s account ID, department, time, count, and result, plus a keyed fingerprint—not employee names or the email body. Microsoft stores sent messages, and Netlify/Microsoft may retain operational logs. People with administrative access to those services control that storage.
