Your schedule is temporary. You stay in control.

Schedule files and recipient settings are processed only in this browser tab. Load the original report already filtered to 15-year-olds; the app does not infer employee ages. The app does not upload them, write them to browser storage, or keep a schedule history. Refreshing, leaving the page, or choosing Clear everything resets the app. Loading a different schedule or worksheet preserves the recipient settings for this tab, while clearing the schedule review.

Copies you intentionally put on the clipboard, download, or send by email remain in those places. Your email provider may keep drafts and sent messages. Hosting sign-in and access logs are separate from schedule processing.
