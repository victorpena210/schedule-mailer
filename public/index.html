Your schedule is temporary. You stay in control.

Schedule files are read and organized in this browser tab. Saved recipient settings are stored privately on the server and returned only after the sending code is checked. Your working recipient settings stay in the tab. Load the original report already filtered to 15-year-olds; the app does not infer employee ages. The original spreadsheet is not uploaded. When you confirm a direct send, the selected department’s names, locations, end times, message, and recipient addresses pass through a Netlify function to Resend for delivery. Reviewing a test summary sends all included department drafts to the private function for validation. Confirming the test sends their combined contents through Resend only to Victor’s work address. The app does not write schedules or the sending code to browser storage, a database, or application logs. Refreshing, leaving the page, or choosing Clear everything resets the app. Loading a different schedule or worksheet preserves the recipient settings for this tab, while clearing the schedule review.

Copies you intentionally put on the clipboard, download, or send by email remain in those places. Email providers may retain sent messages, delivery records, and message content under their account settings. Approved recipient addresses and sending credentials are configured privately in Netlify. Copies of sent emails remain with their recipients. Hosting sign-in and access logs are separate from schedule processing.
