Your schedule is temporary. You stay in control.

Schedule files are read and organized in this browser tab. Saved recipient settings are stored privately on the server and returned only after the sending code is checked. Your working recipient settings stay in the tab. Load the original report already filtered to 15-year-olds; the app does not infer employee ages. The original spreadsheet is not uploaded. When you confirm a direct send, the selected department’s names, locations, end times, message, and recipient addresses pass through a Netlify function to Resend for delivery. Reviewing a test summary sends all included department drafts to the private function for validation. Confirming the test sends their combined contents through Resend only to Victor’s work address. Email history stores department names, recipients, subjects, schedule dates, planned send times, provider references, and a fingerprint used to prevent duplicate sends. It does not store employee rows, email bodies, or your password. History is available only after signing in. The original spreadsheet and password are not stored in browser storage or application logs. Refreshing, leaving the page, or choosing Sign out & clear clears the current upload and signs you out; saved email history remains. Loading a different schedule or worksheet preserves the recipient settings for this tab, while clearing the schedule review.

Copies you intentionally put on the clipboard, download, or send by email remain in those places. Email providers may retain sent messages, delivery records, and message content under their account settings. Default recipient addresses and sending credentials are configured privately in Netlify. Signed-in operators can edit recipients in each daily email. Confirmed scheduled emails are stored by the email provider and still send after this page closes. Copies of sent emails remain with their recipients. Hosting sign-in and access logs are separate from schedule processing.
